# 03 · Seedance-Produktfilme für die Brand-Accounts

Cinematic statt Handy-Look: Diese Filme laufen auf den **bärly-Brand-Accounts** und als **Ads**. Die Avatar-Accounts bleiben bei den UGC-Skripten aus `02-skripte/`.

**Einstellungen in Higgsfield:** Seedance 2.5 · Modus **Omni Reference** · 9:16 (für Feed-Ads zusätzlich 4:5 oder 1:1) · 1080p · Audio an (nur Sounddesign, kein Sprecher) · Referenzbilder: Packshot der Sorte + freigestellter Bär aus `assets/characters/<sorte>.webp`.

Tipp: Erst mit `draft` (480p) testen, den besten Draft dann in 1080p finalisieren. Spart Credits.

Jeder Film hat vier Teile: Shot-Timeline (das ist der Prompt), Effekt-Inventar, Dichte-Karte, Energiebogen. **Für Seedance kopierst du nur den Codeblock „Prompt“.** Der Rest ist für dich zum Verstehen und Nachschneiden.

## P-01 · GLOW „Krone sitzt“ · 15 s

### Prompt

```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic product film, 15 seconds, no dialogue, sound design only. Reference 1: the pink bärly GLOW jar (keep label, logo, colour and shape exactly). Reference 2: the pink GLOW bear with gold crown and heart-shaped sunglasses (keep design exactly).

SHOT 1 (00:00-00:01) — Pink Burst
• EFFECT: Speed ramp (deceleration) + heavy motion blur
• Camera enters mid-whip-pan through a blur of pink and gold light streaks, decelerating into a soft pink studio.
• Transition: the blur resolves directly into shot 2.

SHOT 2 (00:01-00:03) — Jar Hero
• EFFECT: Slow push-in + light sweep
• The pink GLOW jar stands on a glossy marble plinth, a diagonal band of warm light sweeps across the label from left to right, the metallic accents shimmer.
• Camera pushes in slowly at eye level, 85mm look, shallow depth of field.

SHOT 3 (00:03-00:05) — Lid Twist
• EFFECT: Macro + slow-motion (approximately 25% speed)
• Extreme close-up: a hand with a gold ring twists the lid open, the lid lifts out of frame.
• Exit: hard cut on the lid leaving frame.

SHOT 4 (00:05-00:08) — Gummy Rain
• EFFECT: Slow-motion (approximately 20% speed) + rack focus
• Exactly two raspberry-red gummy bears fall in slow motion past the camera onto a pink satin cloth, tiny sparkles catch the light; focus pulls from the first gummy to the second.
• This is the SIGNATURE VISUAL EFFECT.

SHOT 5 (00:08-00:10) — Ingredients
• EFFECT: Clean orbit + floating type
• Camera orbits 45° around the jar, three soft glass droplets float beside it, each reflecting pink light (space for text overlays added later).

SHOT 6 (00:10-00:12) — Bear Reveal
• EFFECT: White bloom flash (entry) + digital zoom pump
• From a soft white bloom, the pink GLOW bear appears next to the jar, adjusts its crown and lowers its heart sunglasses with a wink; the frame punches in slightly and settles.

SHOT 7 (00:12-00:15) — End Card
• EFFECT: Static hold + slow fade of light
• Jar and bear side by side on the marble plinth, pink backdrop, everything still, a last glint runs over the crown. Space at the top for the logo.
Style: premium beauty commercial, pastel pink and gold palette, soft high-key light, crisp product detail, gentle shimmer sounds, a light whoosh on the whip pan, soft chime on the wink.
```
